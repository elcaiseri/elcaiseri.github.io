AI Strategy & Consulting

Strategic roadmap to implement AI across your organization with measurable ROI.

The Problem

Many organizations struggle with AI adoption because they:

  • Don't know where AI can have the biggest impact
  • Lack clarity on build vs. buy decisions
  • Underestimate implementation complexity
  • Can't quantify the ROI of AI initiatives
  • Have had failed AI projects in the past

Without a clear strategy, AI projects often fail to deliver value, wasting time and resources.

The Solution

I provide comprehensive AI strategy consulting that gives you:

  • Clear 12-month AI roadmap with prioritized initiatives
  • Cost-benefit analysis for each AI opportunity
  • Technical feasibility assessments based on your data and infrastructure
  • Build vs. buy recommendations with vendor evaluations
  • Risk mitigation strategies to avoid common pitfalls
