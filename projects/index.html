OpenFPL Scout AI

OpenFPL Scout AI is a live Fantasy Premier League scout built on XGBoost regression and time-series features, trained on historical form and refreshed with official FPL data. It forecasts player points, optimizes a full 15-player squad, and ranks captaincy picks before every gameweek deadline.

Impact: a free public app, rebuilt every gameweek from live data.
