How We Work Together

A structured approach that minimizes risk and delivers measurable results in 90 days or less.

01

Discovery & Alignment

Week 1

We start by deeply understanding your business challenges, objectives, and what success looks like.

  • Deep-dive into business challenges and objectives
  • Technical infrastructure assessment
  • Data readiness evaluation
  • ROI modeling and success metrics definition
Deliverable: Technical feasibility report & project roadmap
02

Solution Design

Weeks 2-3

Translate requirements into a detailed technical architecture and implementation plan.

  • Architecture design and technology stack selection
  • Data pipeline and model design
  • Integration planning with existing systems
  • Security, compliance, and scalability planning
Deliverable: Technical architecture document & implementation plan
03

Development & Training

Weeks 4-8

Build the AI system with regular demos and iterative refinement based on feedback.

  • MVP development with weekly demos
  • Model training and fine-tuning
  • Iterative testing and refinement
  • Performance optimization
Deliverable: Working AI system with documented API
04

Deployment & Launch

Weeks 9-10

Deploy to production with proper testing, monitoring, and team training.

  • Production deployment with staged rollout
  • Real-world testing and validation
  • Team training and documentation
  • Monitoring and alerting setup
Deliverable: Production system with monitoring dashboard
05

Optimization & Support

Ongoing

Continuous improvement based on real-world performance and evolving needs.

  • Performance monitoring and reporting
  • Model retraining and updates
  • Feature enhancements based on usage
  • Technical support and troubleshooting
Deliverable: Monthly performance reports & continuous improvement

Performance Guarantee

If the AI system doesn't meet agreed-upon KPIs within 30 days of production deployment, I'll continue optimization at no additional cost until targets are achieved.
